Title: ICAR-IISR Soybean Girdle Beetle and Semilooper Advisory
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Soybean Research (IISR), Indore
Crop: Soybean (सोयाबीन)
Category: Pest and Disease Management
Pests: Girdle Beetle (Obereopsis brevis / खोडकिडा / चक्री भुंगा) and Tobacco Caterpillar / Green Semilooper (Spodoptera litura)
Regions: Central India (Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan)

Symptoms:
- Girdle Beetle: Adult females make two ring-like cuts (girdles) on the petiole or main stem. The portion above the cut withers, dries, and drops off.
- Semilooper & Caterpillars: Larvae feed vigorously on leaves, leaving only veins intact (skeletonized leaves).

Management & Chemical Controls:
- For Girdle Beetle & Stem Borer:
  - Thiamethoxam 12.6% + Lambda-cyhalothrin 9.5% ZC @ 0.25 ml per liter of water (50 ml in 200 L water per acre).
  - OR Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml per liter of water (60 ml per acre).
- For Defoliators / Semiloopers:
  - Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 gram per liter of water (80 grams per acre).
  - Spray when morning dew has evaporated.

Safety Notice:
- Avoid spraying during active honeybee foraging hours (early morning).
