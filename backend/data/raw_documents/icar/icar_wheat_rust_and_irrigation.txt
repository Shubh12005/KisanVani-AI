Title: ICAR-IIWBR Wheat Rust & Irrigation Advisory
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Wheat and Barley Research (IIWBR), Karnal
Crop: Wheat (गेहूं / गहू)
Category: Disease and Irrigation Management
Disease: Yellow / Stripe Rust (Puccinia striiformis) and Brown Rust (Puccinia triticina)
Regions: North-Western & Central Plains (Punjab, Haryana, UP, Rajasthan, MP)

Rust Disease Symptoms:
- Yellow Rust: Yellow pustules arranged in linear stripes parallel to leaf veins, releasing powdery spores when touched.
- Brown Rust: Round to oval brownish-orange pustules scattered randomly over leaf surface.

Rust Chemical Control (Approved Dosages):
- Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1.0 ml per liter of water (200 ml in 200 liters of water per acre).
- OR Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1.0 ml per liter of water (200 ml per acre).
- Repeat spray after 15 days if cloudy/humid weather persists.

Critical Wheat Irrigation Calendar (6 Stage Plan):
1. Crown Root Initiation (CRI) - 20-25 days after sowing (Most critical: delay causes yield drops of 25-30%).
2. Tillering Stage - 40-45 days after sowing.
3. Late Jointing Stage - 60-65 days after sowing.
4. Flowering Stage - 80-85 days after sowing.
5. Milk Stage - 100-105 days after sowing.
6. Dough Stage - 115-120 days after sowing.

Fertilizer Recommendation:
- Basal: 50 kg Urea, 75 kg DAP, 25 kg MOP per acre.
- First Top Dressing: 25 kg Urea at CRI stage before 1st irrigation.
- Second Top Dressing: 25 kg Urea at Tillering stage before 2nd irrigation.
